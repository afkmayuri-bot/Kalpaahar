Meet The Founder

Dr. Sayali Nahar

Functional Medicine & Clinical Nutrition Expert

Founder & Director of KalpAahar, Dr. Sayali brings 15 years of experience and a clinical, evidence-informed lens to everyday eating — helping clients build plans that fit real life, not just a chart.

Her foundation in Dental Surgery (BDS) and her advanced training in Dietetics, Functional Medicine, and Clinical Nutrition give her a rare, whole-body view of health — one that treats food as medicine and looks beyond symptoms to their underlying causes. Over the years, this has grown into a wide-ranging practice spanning weight management, diabetes and metabolic health, women's health and PCOS, gut health, heart health, child and family nutrition, healthy ageing, and preventive care.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are consultations online or in person?

All consultations are held one-to-one over video call, so you can join from anywhere at a time that suits your schedule.

How soon will I see results?

Because plans are built for sustainable change rather than crash dieting, most clients notice improvements in energy and habits within a few weeks, with fuller results building over 2-3 months.

Can you help with a medical condition like diabetes or PCOS?

Yes — Dr. Sayali works alongside your doctor to build nutrition plans that support conditions like diabetes, PCOS, thyroid issues, and more, based on your medical history.

What if I have a busy schedule?

Every plan is designed around your real routine and food preferences — not a generic chart that assumes unlimited free time.

Can I cancel or change my program anytime?

Absolutely. Programs are billed monthly with no long-term lock-in, and you can upgrade, downgrade, or pause anytime.
