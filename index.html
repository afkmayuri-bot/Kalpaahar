Meet The Founder

Dr. Sayali Nahar

Functional Medicine & Clinical Nutrition Expert

Founder & Director of KalpAahar, Dr. Sayali brings 15 years of experience and a clinical, evidence-informed lens to everyday eating — helping clients build plans that fit real life, not just a chart.

Her advanced training in Dietetics, Functional Medicine, and Clinical Nutrition enables her to take a comprehensive, whole-person approach to health. Rather than focusing solely on symptoms, she seeks to understand the underlying factors that influence an individual's well-being, recognizing the complex interplay between nutrition, metabolism, lifestyle, environment, and overall health.

Guided by evidence-based functional medicine principles, she combines the science of clinical nutrition with personalized care to identify the root causes of health concerns and develop tailored nutrition and lifestyle strategies. She believes food is a powerful therapeutic tool to heal & optimize health.

Her practice is dedicated to helping individuals achieve better health through personalized, preventive, and sustainable care.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are calls online or in person?

All consultations are conducted remotely via secure audio or video calls, offering convenient and personalised care from the comfort of your home.

What if I have a busy schedule?

Every plan is designed around your real routine and food preferences — not a generic chart that assumes unlimited free time.

How is functional nutrition different from a regular diet plan?

A regular diet plan typically focuses on what to eat. Functional nutrition goes deeper — understanding why your body is responding the way it is, and addressing the root cause through food, lifestyle, and daily habits together.

How soon can I expect to see results?

It depends on your condition and consistency. Most people notice meaningful changes within 3 to 4 weeks — energy, digestion, and sleep often improve first, followed by more specific health markers over time.

How often will my plan be reviewed and adjusted?

In the Guided Wellness Programs, your plan is reviewed and adjusted every week as your body responds and progresses.

Do I need to share my blood reports before booking?

Not mandatory. If you have recent reports, sharing them helps us build a more targeted plan. If not, we work with your detailed health history to get started.

Do I need to stop my current medications or supplements?

No. Nutrition works alongside your medical treatment, never in place of it. Any changes to medication are always your doctor's decision.

Are the ebooks a replacement for consultation?

No, the ebooks are general recipe guides for everyday use. Consultations and programs are personalised to you.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes, anytime.

How many follow-ups do I get?

Follow-ups are included only in the Guided Wellness Programs, with weekly check-ins throughout your 4, 8, or 12-week journey. The Quick Consult and Condition-Specific Nutrition Plan are single focused sessions.
